Squeek Passport

Paste a wallet address and get its Squeekchain passport: Squeeks, power, stakes, rewards and the stamps it has earned.

Holder badges you earned (optional)

Reads public data from the Squeekchain API (Sepolia testnet, about 12 blocks behind the chain). Testnet Squeeks and $SQK have no monetary value. Genesis rank, Aura and badges are optional and self-reported, not verified. Unofficial community tool.
